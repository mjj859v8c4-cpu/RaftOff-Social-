Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

RaftOff Social (“RaftOff”) is a location-aware social app for lake communities. We collect only what we need to operate the service, keep people safe, and improve the product.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: email, display name, username, optional profile/boat photos
  • Content you post: check-ins, messages, photos, events, comments, likes
  • Approximate location only when you Drop Anchor or grant foreground location permission
  • Device & usage analytics (with consent where required) and crash logs
  • Safety data: reports, blocks, moderation actions

3. How we use information

To provide map/feed/check-in/event features, authenticate sessions, enforce guidelines, and improve reliability. Optional commercial insights are place-level aggregates only — never private GPS fishing holes.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal GPS tracks. We may share data with infrastructure providers under contract, law enforcement when required, and other users according to the audience/precision you choose.

5. Retention & choices

Check-ins expire automatically. You can change precision, end check-ins, block/report users, withdraw analytics consent, or request deletion at privacy@raftoff.social.

6. Children

RaftOff is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Contact: privacy@raftoff.social
