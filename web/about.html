The story

It started where the sandbars fill up.

Every summer on Lake St. Clair, the same question hit the group chat: who’s out, where’s the raft-up, and is there room for one more boat? Texts got lost. Pins went cold. The vibe moved before anyone could pull up.

DJ and CJ got tired of guessing. They wanted a live map of the lake scene — Drop Anchor when you’re on the water, see who’s nearby, and find your crew without burning half the day on scavenger-hunt DMs. RaftOff Social is that answer: built first for St. Clair, made for boat owners and everyone they want onboard.
