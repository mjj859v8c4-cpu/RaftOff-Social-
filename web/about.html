The story

One late-night idea. Three years. Then the right tech guy.

CJ thought of RaftOff in bed one day — that half-asleep hit when the lake’s calling and you’re already planning the weekend. Three years later he met DJ, an expert in technology who could turn the vision into something you actually open on the water.

Together they created RaftOff Social because of their love for lakes, parties, and RaftOff on Lake St. Clair. Live maps. Drop Anchor. Find your crew. Same energy that fills the sandbars — now with a signal.
