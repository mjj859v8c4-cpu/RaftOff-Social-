The story

It started with one idea that wouldn’t leave.

CJ thought of RaftOff in bed one day — that quiet moment when the weekend already feels alive and you’re mapping the lake in your head. Three years later he met DJ, an expert in technology who knew how to turn that vision into something you open on the water.

Together they created RaftOff Social because of their love for lakes, parties, and rafting off on Lake St. Clair. They’re the first users. The founders. The ones who made Drop Anchor, live maps, and finding your crew feel as natural as pulling up to the sandbar.
