Community Guidelines

Keep RaftOff fun, respectful, and safe.

Be a good neighbor

  • No harassment, hate, threats, or doxxing
  • No sexual content involving minors
  • No spam, scams, or fake check-ins

Respect privacy on the water

  • Don’t expose private fishing spots or home docks without consent
  • Prefer location / approximate precision for public map presence
  • Use Block if someone won’t leave you alone

Safety first

  • RaftOff is not navigation — use official charts
  • Don’t encourage reckless or impaired operation

Enforcement

We may hide content, warn, suspend, or ban accounts. Report violations in-app. Appeals: trust@raftoff.social.
