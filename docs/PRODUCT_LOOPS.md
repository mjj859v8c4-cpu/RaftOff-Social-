# Product Loops

Three loops that define RaftOff Social. Ship these before expanding scope.

## 1. Discover people → Connect → Meet on the water

Find boaters on your lake through profiles, discover, and crews. Connect first in-app; the payoff is showing up at the same sandbar, marina, or raft-up.

## 2. See activity → Drop Anchor → Join the action

"What’s Hot" and live check-ins show where energy is building. Drop Anchor at a spot, show up, and join what’s already happening.

## 3. Meet on the lake → Connect on RaftOff → Boat together again

Weekend chemistry on the water becomes lasting connections. Message, crew up, and plan the next run without losing the thread between Saturdays.

---

## Densification: Lake St. Clair first

Win one lake before spreading thin.

- Seed verified hotspots, dining partners, and demo/live check-in density on Lake St. Clair.
- Make "Hot on Lake St. Clair" the default mental model for visitors and early users.
- Expand to additional Michigan lakes only after St. Clair has repeatable weekly activity and clear social proof.
